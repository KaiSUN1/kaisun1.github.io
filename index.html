👋 About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Monash University, supervised by A/Prof. Levin Kuhlmann and Dr. Peibo Duan. My research focuses on efficient deep learning for computer vision, including brain-inspired neural networks. Previously, I received a Master of Engineering in Transportation Planning and Management at Hohai University, working on metro passenger-flow forecasting with graph neural networks and on optimization for logistics routing and network design.
