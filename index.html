About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Data Science and AI at Monash University, supervised by Prof. Levin Kuhlmann and Ben Duan. My earlier research focused on algorithmic methods for transportation systems, where I worked with traditional optimization techniques and graph-based approaches. My current research focuses on computer vision and efficient brain-inspired learning, with an emphasis on improving the accuracy and computational efficiency of image classification through knowledge distillation, neuron design, and model compression.
