Energy-efficient brain-inspired AI

About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Information Technology at Monash University. My research focuses on energy-efficient brain-inspired artificial intelligence, especially spiking neural networks, knowledge distillation and biologically inspired neuron design. In the longer term, I aim to bring these efficiency principles to large-scale reasoning systems.

Research Interests

Spiking neural networks · Knowledge distillation · Neuromorphic computing · Efficient deep learning · Brain-inspired intelligence
